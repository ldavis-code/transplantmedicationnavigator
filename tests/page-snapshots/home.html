Not sure whether you're on the brand or the generic?

Most transplant patients are on generics and were never told. Your bottle holds the answer, and it decides which options are yours.

Your label says “tacrolimus”

Lowercase drug name, often with a manufacturer like Sandoz or Accord. This is the generic.

→ Compare cash prices. No copay card exists.

Your label says “Prograf”

Capitalized brand name. Same medicine, different price, different programs.

→ Copay card or manufacturer assistance.

We never tell you to change your medication. That's your transplant team's decision — including switching between brand and generic, or between generic manufacturers. We show you what the medicine they prescribed should cost you.

Struggling with more than the cost?

Being a transplant patient can be hard on your feelings. You can get free, private help any time, day or night.

988

National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

24/7 • Confidential

Asking for help shows strength, not weakness. Life after a transplant is hard on your body and your feelings. Taking care of how you feel is just as important as taking your medicine. Having a hard time? Please reach out. We want to help you.

Call or Text

Dial or text 988 from any phone (Spanish-language support: press 2 / oprima el 2)
